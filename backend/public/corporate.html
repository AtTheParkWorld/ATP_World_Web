05 · Private Sessions
5 / 11
The mechanic, concretely

Your sessions are invisible to everyone else.

This isn't a calendar invite or a WhatsApp group. It's built into the platform. Private company sessions live in the same app your staff already use for public ATP sessions — they're just gated to your company.

1

Your employee opens the ATP app

Same app, same login, no separate corporate portal to learn or support.

2

Your sessions appear alongside the public ones

Marked with your company badge and logo, so it's obvious which sessions are yours.

3

Nobody outside the company sees them

The session simply doesn't exist for non-staff — not in the list, not in search, not bookable by link. Logged-out visitors never see it at all.

4

Off-boarding is instant

Remove someone from your company account and the private sessions vanish from their app on the next refresh.
