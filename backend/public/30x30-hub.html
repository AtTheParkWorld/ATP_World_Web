About the Event

A 30-day fitness movement at Dubai Internet City

As part of Dubai Fitness Challenge, the Dubai Internet City 30x30 Fitness Hub turns the community into a wellness destination for a whole month.

Curated by At The Park with Dubai Internet City, the programme brings free daily sessions — running, yoga, strength and conditioning, dance and mindfulness — led by professional coaches, plus the 30x30 Challenge Series and the Relay Rumble.

It all starts on the morning of Saturday 31 October with the Pink Run.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Every session at the 30x30 Fitness Hub and all four challenges are free — you just need to book your spot.

Do I need an ATP account?

Yes, a free one. It's how we save your spot, give you your check-in QR code and add your challenge results.

If I book here, is it in the app too?

Yes. The website and the ATP app share the same booking system — book in one, it shows up in the other with the same QR code.

How do the challenge qualifiers work?

Each challenge has qualifier slots in the challenge calendar. Book a slot to register — one slot per day per challenge. You can try on several days: every attempt counts and your best result ranks. Results are checked and published by the ATP team.

How do I register on the same team as my colleagues?

One colleague is the captain: they open a team challenge slot, name the team and book. They get an invite link to share (WhatsApp or copy). Teammates open the link, sign in with their own free ATP account, join and book the team’s slot. A team’s result is the total of its members’ results.

Where does the charity money go?

Challenges with a charity cause show it on the challenge. After you register you’re asked — optionally — if you’d like to donate; donations are made directly on the charity’s own page, so 100% goes to the cause.

Who makes the final?

Each challenge has a finals cut — for example the top 15 men and top 15 women, or the top 10 teams — shown on the leaderboard. You can’t register for the final directly: you get there through the qualifiers.
