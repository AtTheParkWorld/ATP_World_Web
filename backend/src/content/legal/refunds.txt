Most of what ATP offers is free, so this policy is short. It covers the things you can actually pay for: memberships, paid sessions, and store orders.

Free sessions:

Nothing to refund. Just cancel your booking if you cannot make it, so someone else can take the space.

Paid sessions and coaching:

Cancel more than 12 hours before the session starts and we refund you in full, whether you paid by card or with points. Cancel within 12 hours and we cannot refund the place, because the coach and the court are already committed. If ATP cancels the session, or moves it to a time you cannot make, you get a full refund or a credit, whichever you prefer.

One-to-one coaching works differently:

Private coaching sessions are booked directly with a coach who sets aside that time for you, so the notice period is longer. Cancel 24 hours or more before and you are refunded in full. Cancel between 2 and 24 hours before and you are refunded half, with the remainder going to the coach for the time they held. Inside 2 hours there is no refund.

Memberships:

You can cancel a paid membership at any time from your profile. Your benefits continue until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again after that.

We do not refund part-used months as a matter of course. If you have just been charged for a new period and you have not used any member benefits in it, email us within 14 days and we will refund it.

If you were charged by mistake, for example twice for the same period, tell us and we will put it right in full.

Store orders:

Orders from the ATP store are covered by the returns and refund terms shown at checkout. In general, unworn items in their original condition can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Delivery costs are not refundable unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong thing.

Points and partner offers:

Points are deducted when you generate a discount code, not when you use it. Points are not money and are not refundable for cash.

If you generated a code and never used it, email us with the code. Where the partner can confirm it was not redeemed, we will return the points to your balance.

If a partner does not honour a valid code, tell us and we will either sort it out with them or return your points.

How to ask for a refund:

Email general@atthepark.world with your name, the email on your ATP account, what you paid for and when, and what went wrong. We aim to reply within two working days.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid from. Once we approve a refund, your bank usually takes five to ten working days to show it.
