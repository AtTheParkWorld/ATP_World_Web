Most ATP sessions are free. That is the point of what we do, and we intend to keep it that way.

Physical activity carries risk. Before you train with us, please read this carefully:

You take part voluntarily. You are responsible for deciding whether you are fit enough to take part in any session, and for stopping if you feel unwell.

You confirm that you are in good enough health to exercise, and that you have told us about any condition or injury that could affect your safety or the safety of others.

Our coaches are there to lead the session, not to supervise your medical condition. They are not responsible for diagnosing an injury or illness.

If you feel unwell during a session, stop and tell the coach immediately.

## Booking and attending sessions

Spaces are limited and bookings are first come, first served. If a session is full you may join the waitlist and we will let you know if a space opens.

Please cancel if you cannot make it. Showing up matters, and a space you do not use is a space someone else could have had. Repeatedly booking and not attending may lead us to limit your bookings.

You are checked in at a session by an ATP coach or ambassador. Only a check-in counts as attendance.

For sports with skill levels, such as padel, you must choose the level that honestly reflects your ability. Sessions and courts may be restricted to certain levels so that games are safe and enjoyable for everyone.

We may cancel or move a session, for example for weather, venue availability or safety. We will tell you as early as we can.
